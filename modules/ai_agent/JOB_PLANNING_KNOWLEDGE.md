# Job & Planning Module — Knowledge Base Reference

## Module Context
This knowledge base covers queries related to the Live Production Floor, Job Planning Board, and Job Cards.
It is activated strictly via the `/job` or `/planning` commands.

## Keywords Triggers
`job`, `planning`, `floor`, `production`, `card`, `brc`, `jmb`, `pck`, `work`, `status`

## User Questions — Category Wise

### 1. Production Floor Status
- "ফ্লোর প্রোডাকশনের অবস্থা কী?" (What is the floor production status?)
- "Which jobs are currently running on the floor?"
- "আজকের প্রোডাকশন টার্গেট কত?" (What is today's production target?)
- "মেশিন ১-এ এখন কোন কাজ চলছে?" (Which job is running on Machine 1 right now?)

### 2. Job Card & BRC Details
- "১০৫ নম্বর জবের BRC ডিটেইলস দেখাও" (Show BRC details for job 105)
- "What are the specs for Jobcard 230?"
- "JMB নাম্বার ৪৫৬ এর আপডেট কী?" (What is the update on JMB 456?)
- "কোন জবের কাজ পেন্ডিং আছে?" (Which jobs are pending?)

### 3. Planning & Assignment
- "XYZ অপারেটরকে কোন কোন কাজ দেওয়া হয়েছে?" (Which jobs are assigned to operator XYZ?)
- "Show planning board for today"
- "কোন জবের কাজ কালকের জন্য শিডিউল করা আছে?" (Which jobs are scheduled for tomorrow?)
- "Unassigned job-এর লিস্ট দাও" (Give me the list of unassigned jobs)

## KB Training Tips

Add these common combinations as Quick Chip entries in your AI Settings:
- `Live Floor Status` → Shows active running jobs on the production floor.
- `Pending Jobs` → Lists all jobs waiting to be processed.
- `Operator Workload` → Shows job assignment per operator.
